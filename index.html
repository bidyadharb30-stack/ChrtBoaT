Android (Chrome Browser)
  1. Browser ke upar right corner mein 3 dots (⋮) par tap karein.
  2. "Install app" ya "Add to Home screen" select karein.
  3. Install par click karein — CoupleSync icon aapke phone menu mein aa jayega!
iPhone / iOS (Safari Browser)
  1. Niche beech mein Share icon (□↑) par tap karein.
  2. Scroll karke "Add to Home Screen" par click karein.
  3. Upar right mein Add dabayein — full-screen app ready!
